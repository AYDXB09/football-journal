## 2. Accounts and Roles

The Service has four roles: **Player**, **Parent**, **Coach**, and **Admin**.

- **Player** accounts hold the primary record — match/training data, reflections, goals, and
  photos.
- **Parent** accounts, once linked to a Player, can read everything the player sees and add their
  own observation notes.
- **Coach** accounts, once invited by a player or parent, get either `limited` access (aggregated
  stats, diagnostics, goals) or `full` access (everything except entries marked
  `player_only`), depending on what was granted.
- You're responsible for keeping your own account credentials secure, and for the accuracy of
  information you enter — including information about other people, covered in Section 4 below.

## 3. The AI Coach — Experimental, On by Default, Toggleable

Match reflections, season reviews, and summer plans can be sent to an AI coaching feature that
returns coach-voice feedback. Unlike a bring-your-own-key tool, **this happens server-side using
the Service's own connection to Google's Gemini API** — you don't need (and can't provide) your own
key, but it also means the operator of this Service, not you, controls that data flow. **This
feature can be turned off** in Setup → Preferences — when off, the Service's own servers refuse the
request rather than just hiding the button, so no reflection content is sent to Gemini at all while
it's disabled. AI feedback is a supportive tool, not professional coaching advice, and may be
wrong, generic, or miss context a real coach would catch.

**This feature is experimental.** It is not a core, permanent part of the Service, and the operator
may change, limit, or remove it entirely — for any reason, including cost, reliability, or a
change in AI provider — without that being treated as a reduction in the Service you're otherwise
entitled to. If the AI coach is removed, existing AI feedback text already saved on your past
entries is not affected, but no new feedback will be generated.

## 4. Entering Information About Teammates

The Service lets a player record teammates by name and nickname (for @mentions in reflections).
**Only name/nickname are collected** — no phone number, email, or Instagram handle, specifically
because a teammate has no account here and never consents to their own contact details being
stored by someone else. Teammate records are private to the player who created them; a teammate
does not get their own account or visibility into this data through this feature.
